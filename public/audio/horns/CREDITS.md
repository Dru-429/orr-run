The motorcycle recording uses Freesound’s publicly supplied high-quality MP3 preview. The other downloads are BigSoundBank MP3s. Files were trimmed, filtered, converted to mono PCM, level-matched and crossfaded for sustained playback. See `sources.json` for download links, exact trim/loop intervals and SHA-256 checksums; `scripts/prepare-horns.py` reproduces the edits from downloaded originals.

Fifteen gameplay variants share these six recordings. Auto-rickshaw sounds are pitch adaptations of electric car horns. Bus and motorcycle recordings are not authenticated BMTC or Bengaluru fleet sounds. These are game sound design, not a sound survey of this corridor.
